About us

Founded in Portugal, made for Europe.

Traços Fidalgos is a Portuguese textile atelier founded in 2017 in Perafita, Matosinhos. We develop and manufacture garments and textile pieces with technical rigour, close follow-up and a premium sense of detail.

Our value: precision, discretion and consistent finishing.
