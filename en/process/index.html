Process

From brief to final piece, with method and detail.

01

Briefing and references

We receive images, drawings, sample garments, technical sheets or a simple description of what needs to be developed. At this stage we define purpose, use, quantities, materials and finishing level.

02

Technical analysis

We assess construction, proportions, fabric, details, sewing complexity and the best production path. When necessary, we suggest adjustments to improve result, cost and feasibility.

03

Prototype or sample

We develop the first piece to test fit, structure, measurements, finishes and visual reading. This is the ideal stage to correct before production.

04

Production preparation

Once the piece is approved, we organise materials, sewing sequence, instructions, quality control and final details to maintain consistency.

05

Sewing and control

Production is followed with attention to finishing, proportions, labels, pressing and final presentation.

06

Delivery and continuity

We deliver the finished project and leave a clear basis for future replenishments, new capsules, adjustments or development of other pieces.

Outcome

What the client receives

  • clarity on stages and decisions
  • close follow-up
  • attention to materials and finishes
  • production in Portugal with technical responsibility
  • a result suited to the brand, company or special project
