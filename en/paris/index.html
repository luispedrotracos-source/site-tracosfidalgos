Traços Fidalgos

Portuguese fashion production partner for demanding clients.

Traços Fidalgos is a Portuguese atelier in Perafita, Matosinhos, dedicated to developing and manufacturing garments and textile projects for brands, companies and professional clients.

We work from brief review and prototyping through production, finishing, quality control and preparation of the final piece. The goal is simple: turn ideas into product with method, attention to detail and presentation suited to the European market.

Services

What we do

01

Prototyping and development

Support in turning an idea, drawing, reference or technical sheet into a physical piece ready to test and adjust.

02

Small and medium series

Flexible production for brands, boutiques, capsule collections and projects that do not require huge industrial volumes.

03

Uniforms and premium pieces

Uniforms, custom pieces and textile projects with careful image, consistency and professional finishing.

04

Special textile projects

Scarves, accessories, bespoke pieces, delicate materials and proposals with their own visual identity.

Value

Why work with Traços Fidalgos

This presentation shows directly what the company offers: production in Portugal, proximity, flexibility, sewing quality and direct follow-up.

  • Production in Portugal, close to the main European markets.
  • Technical rigour in construction, finishing and understanding of materials.
  • Capability for delicate pieces, capsule collections, premium uniforms and custom projects.
  • Direct communication with an experienced team used to working with demanding brands and projects.

Materials

Materials, identity and finishing

The Paris booklet shows different areas of work: lace and fluid fabrics, pieces with identity, custom scarves, bespoke textile pieces and proposals such as 320 g/m² diagonal fleece. On the site, these themes are explained as concrete services and capabilities, not just presentation pages.
